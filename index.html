tgkimi

tgkimi is a personal automation toolkit. It is a private OAuth application that lets the owner's own local tools and AI assistants access their own Google account data — Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs and Sheets — exclusively through official Google APIs.

Who uses it

Access is limited to the owner's own Google accounts. This is not a public service: there is no sign-up, no data collection from other users, and no website accounts. Google OAuth is used only so that the owner can grant their own tools permission to work with their own data.

What it does

Typical uses: reading and organising the owner's mail, managing calendar events, reading and editing the owner's documents, and preparing personal summaries and reports. All processing happens locally on the owner's computer.
