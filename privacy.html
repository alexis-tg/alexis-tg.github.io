tgkimi — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

tgkimi is a personal OAuth application used by the owner's local automation tools and AI assistants to access their own Google account data through official Google APIs.

What data is accessed

  • Gmail — read and send mail;
  • Google Calendar — read and create events;
  • Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets — read, create and edit files;
  • basic profile information (email address) needed to identify the account.

Access is granted only by the account owner through Google's OAuth consent screen, and covers only the accounts that explicitly grant permission (the owner's own accounts).

How the data is used

  • To provide automation features for the owner: reading and organising mail, managing calendar events, reading and editing documents, preparing personal summaries and reports.
  • No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.
  • No data is used for advertising.
  • No data is used to train machine-learning models.

Where the data is stored

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer. Message and document content is processed in memory during a task and is not persistently stored by the application. There are no external servers operated by this application.

Retention and revoking access

Access remains valid until revoked. The owner (or any user who granted access) can revoke it at any time in Google Account settings: Security → Third-party apps with account access → remove access. After revocation, all locally stored tokens stop working.
